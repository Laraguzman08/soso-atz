↓ ABOUT ATEEZ

The group consists of eight members: Hongjoong, Seonghwa, Yunho, Yeosang, San, Mingi, Wooyoung, and Jongho. They debuted on October 24, 2018, with the EP "Treasure EP.1: All to Zero". ATEEZ is known for their powerful performances, unique concepts, and strong connection with their fanbase, ATINY. They have gained international recognition and have a growing discography that includes multiple albums and singles.
